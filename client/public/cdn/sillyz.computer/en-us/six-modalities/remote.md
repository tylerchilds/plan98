## Remote

<cdn-video src="/public/cdn/sillyz.computer/six-modalities/Remote/manifest.m3u8"></cdn-video>

In this video, we show a QR code on one device. We then scan the QR code to be able to control that device from a remote device.

To feel the implications of this demo, jump on a video call with a friend and then follow these instructions.

1. Pull up this page
2. Share your screen
3. Click the QR code below
4. Have your friend scan the resulting QR code
5. Your friend now controls your screen.
