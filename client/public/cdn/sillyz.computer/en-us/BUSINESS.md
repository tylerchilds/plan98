# Business Model

A single representative to manage customer support requests, lift 50 lbs, and fulfill merchandise pickups and returns.

**Two Year Runway Per Employee**

```
1 person working
12 hours per week for
52 weeks per year at
2 times the minimum wage of
7.25 federally at this time
18,096 total investment for a
2,088 down payment to unlock
1 sponsorship unit fully vested
```

### Example:

`14.50 Currency Units * 12 Time Units * 52 Space Units * 2 years`

> In Massachusetts in 2004, the minimum wage was $7.25 per hour. A counter offer to be employed as a junior computer teller by any corporation for any industry would have been $14.50.


In 2004 over two years, outside administrative overhead, the cost to the employer and the benefit to employee, a.k.a. the exchange of services, would be $18,096. The services rendered would be 12 hours of active civic engagement.

#### BOTTOM LINE

2025 US Federal Minimum Wage: $7.25

Investment Units are $18,096 to be distributed over two years.

With an up front first month, last month, and security deposit, the total down payment is $2,088 to begin funding the future generations of computer tellers.

Disbursements are weekly at $174.

## Role Responsibilities

Answer general product questions as well as provide technical demonstrations of core product features to both individuals and groups.

### Role Location

Libraries, computer labs, internet cafes, maker spaces-- where access to compute is permitted and encouraged.

#### Title

Computer Teller

##### Instructions

Hey kid. Turns out only the internet thrives on negativity.

In business and in life, people gravitate towards positive experiences. Our business is that business.

By showing up and being a helpful person, people are naturally going to want to get all up in our business and how we do business.

Our mission is to democratize access to compute. To ensure no kid gets left behind in the computer revolution. And everyone is a kid at heart, so we can't discriminate by age, anymore.

Steve Jobs once called the computer a bicycle for the mind.

Our job is to help people ride bikes and then they'll be riding and customizing their bike forever.

Your mission, should you choose to accept it, is to become a valued member of your community for your intellect and wit.

From that, people will purchase the platform and product privately, funding the pragmatic prototypes we play  publicly.

###### Benefits

- Flexible Hours (Just post a schedule two weeks in advance that overlaps with "banking" hours)
- Flexible Location (Just a publicly accessible institution where hands on support is in short supply)

After leaving this job, you will have established a number of personal contacts that will carry you through life.

You will have earned enough cash to cover basic living expenses while leaving space and time for your priorities.

## Day to Day Life

You meet with people.

Your challenges are the challenges of your constituents-- the people that have decided they want to be told how to tell their computers to do what they're told by a professional: you!

You are their friendly, neighborhood computer teller!

Honestly, this is just formalizing the paper work and the career path that you have already demonstrated the aptitude for by being free tech-support at every family gathering ever.
