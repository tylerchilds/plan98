Plan98: The Card Game

Economy. Factory. Victory.

Rules

Player Setup:

1. Build Computer Deck (max of 20 software, 20 hardware, and 20 event Cards)
2. Place 5 software cards in each player's PASSIVE STAGING
3. Draw 7 cards

Game start:

1. Cut decks to resolve initiative initiative; highest level gains initiative and reveals first.
2. Each player may reveal a higher level card from their hand to gain initiative.

Turn:

1. Upkeep: move ACTIVE SOFTWARE to PASSIVE, deactivate HARDWARE, draw card
2. Activate: from hand, then staging, then production
3. Resolve defenses (3+: Attack Left, Defend Right)

End:

1. Null Pointer: Player cannot draw a card
2. Empty Call Stack: All hardware has been eliminated from production and all software has been eliminated from staging

Clarifications:

1. No max hand, no reshuffle
2. Attacking hardware may not defend until next upkeep

Card Structure

Power: Lightning, Ocean, Volcano, Forest, Atmosphere
Cost: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9
Level: Byte, Kilo, Mega, Giga, Tera, Peta, Exo, Zetta, Yotta
Type: Software, Hardware, Event

Types

Software:

Your life force. Run out of software, you lose.

Activate in STAGING area providing COST to POOL

Activate at COST from POOL to play from HAND to STAGING

Hardware:

Activate at COST from POOL to play from HAND to STAGING

In STAGING, may be activated to provide COST to POOL
In PRODUCTION, may be activated to ATTACK
In DEFENSE, may be activated to DEFEND

Events:

May be played at any time and are resolved in the order they are declared

1. Spawn: Play HARDWARE of COST or less from HAND to PRODUCTION
2. Destroy: Destroy TARGET hardware of COST or less
3. Cancel: Nullify EVENT of COST or less
4. Hot Swap: Equip to TARGET HARDWARE. Target is now LEVEL level COST cost.

Resolution

Attacks will inflict damage equal to their COST.
Defending players choose how to defend. Higher power levels win (B<K<M<G<T<P<E<Z<Y) (elemental immunities) 
All damage from a single attacker is blocked by a single defender regardless of power differential.

Notes:

Software can be any misc. artwork, media, code, poetry, resource.
Favorites will just be named colors, firebrick, darkorange, gold, mediumseagreen, slateblue, mediumpurple

Hardware will scale from

Circuits, to Chips, to Computers, to Clusters, to Networks, to Nations, to Corporations, to Religions, to Planets.
